# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring teams and technical peers evaluating Abdullah as a backend engineer who can also identify ideas and ship complete products. Potential collaborators and product users are secondary audiences.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio should prove backend engineering depth and a maker mindset, then give interested visitors a direct way to start a conversation.

## Positioning

Abdullah combines backend engineering with hands-on product delivery. The portfolio should connect technical decisions to working products instead of presenting engineering and making as separate identities.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from a job application, LinkedIn, GitHub, a shared product link, or an open-source repository. They need to understand Abdullah's focus quickly, inspect credible work, and reach him without searching across the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The portfolio remains a web experience.
- Backend work and maker-led product work both need clear representation.
- The primary action is starting a conversation.
- Project evidence can include live product links and public source repositories.
- Claims without supporting evidence should not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Abdullah Nettoor.
- The voice should be direct, technically credible, and personal.
- Existing product names and factual career details should be preserved unless Abdullah updates them.
- Keep the portfolio minimal without making every section visually identical. Variety should come from the character and content of each project, not from adding more interface chrome.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live product links, including Unigro.
- Public GitHub repositories and open-source work.
- A public Raycast extension pull request.
- Existing biography, skill, career, and contact content in `index.html`.
- Existing avatar assets under `assets/`.
- No confirmed metrics, testimonials, client logos, or detailed case-study material yet. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Show proof before listing tools.
- Explain the engineering behind shipped work.
- Make backend depth and product ownership feel like one coherent practice.
- Keep the route to contact visible and low-friction.
- Prefer a few substantial projects over a long repository catalogue.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should support keyboard and touch interaction, retain native pointer behavior where needed, respect reduced-motion preferences, and remain readable across mobile and desktop layouts.
